03HOW I THINK

The strongest work sees the whole system.

Creativity helps an idea connect. Technology gives it a working form. Strategy gives it direction, while data helps us understand what is happening and where to improve.

Security belongs in that same conversation: it protects the people, information and trust an experience depends on. I’m interested in bringing these perspectives together instead of treating them as separate disciplines.
