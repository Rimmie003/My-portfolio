About me

I’m a Computer Science undergraduate exploring the intersection of technology, digital marketing, creativity, and data. I’m passionate about building digital experiences that combine strong visual design with technology, strategy, and measurable results. My work spans digital marketing, UI/UX design, web development, data analytics, and cybersecurity. I enjoy creating websites and digital campaigns that are visually engaging, user-focused, and built with a strong understanding of how technology works behind the scenes. From developing interactive web experiences and designing intuitive interfaces to analyzing data and exploring cybersecurity, I’m interested in every layer of the digital ecosystem. I believe great digital work sits at the intersection of creativity and technology. Design captures attention, marketing creates connection, data provides insight, development brings ideas to life, and cybersecurity helps protect the experiences we build. Whether I’m working with a small business, an individual brand, or a larger organization, my goal is to create digital solutions that are purposeful, engaging, secure, and backed by thoughtful strategy. I’m constantly experimenting, learning, and developing new ways to turn ideas into digital experiences that make an impact.
